# Illustration System

Choose one primary language and one small supporting language. The source photo supplies recognition anchors; this system supplies the rendering logic.

## Primary languages

| ID | Language | Construction | Best anchors |
|---|---|---|---|
| I01 | airy line engraving | sparse contour, parallel hatch, broad untouched paper | formal architecture, lake, garden, portrait-memory |
| I02 | dense woodcut | carved black/colour masses, white cuts, coarse shadow blocks | mountain, cave, waterfall, dramatic building, food vessel |
| I03 | naïve colour souvenir | uneven decorative outline, simplified perspective, playful flat fills | local street, folk figure, animal, food, intimate scenic memory |
| I04 | institutional screenprint | large flat field, emblem silhouette, white knockout, minimal detail | museum, zoo, aquarium, transport, iconic building |
| I05 | recreation-era graphic | exaggerated contour, outlined display integration, dynamic bands | city energy, amusement, night scene, portrait travel memory |
| I06 | map/schema | route lines, stairs, gates, arrows, index dots, small icons | garden, trail, complex, neighbourhood, food route |
| I07 | scientific/specimen plate | isolated object, labelled inset, fine taxonomy-like drawing | food dish, museum object, animal, plant, vehicle |
| I08 | cut-paper silhouette | one dominant dark field cut by paper-negative shapes | skyline, monument, waterfall, portrait profile |
| I09 | painterly insert | visibly printed miniature painting with simplified tonal zones | mountain corridor, tower, atmospheric water scene |
| I10 | calligraphic landscape | brush title shares space with sparse ink scene; image may be subordinate | poetic landscape, temple, night lake, personal memory |
| I11 | modular vignette grid | two to four unequal picture cells with different crops/scales | multi-anchor city, food ingredients, garden features |
| I12 | cartoon emblem | one mascot-like creature/person plus graphic environment | aquarium, zoo, playful food, family travel |
| I13 | halftone reproduction | one-colour coarse photographic/printed tonal plate, framed as archival insert | documentary monument or personal memory when a photo cue is useful |
| I14 | ornamental collage | figure silhouette, bamboo/cloud/medallion motifs, asymmetric decorative ground | historical/cultural subject, portrait memory, theatre |

## Recomposition rules

- Reduce the source to three to seven masses: landmark, ground/water, vegetation/architecture, one human/animal/food anchor, and optional administrative zone.
- Preserve identity through two to four unmistakable anchors. Discard exact lens perspective, lighting, shadows, leaf count, masonry texture, and object-by-object placement.
- For a portrait-memory source, choose one of: `small traveller within panorama`, `editorial half-figure`, `cut-paper silhouette`, `portrait medallion`, or `cartoon emblem`. Never paste a realistic person over an old background.
- For food, choose one of: `specimen plate`, `vessel woodcut`, `ingredient grid`, `naïve dining scene`, or `flat emblem`. Do not turn it into packaging or a restaurant menu.
- For city streets, compress signs and buildings into emblematic blocks. Do not reproduce every shop sign or illegible pseudo-text.
- For night scenes, translate light into knockout paper, red/yellow accent blocks, or blue field contrast. Do not use neon gradients.

## Supporting marks

Use at most one supporting language:

- small seal or medallion;
- simple route arrow or gate icon;
- botanical corner silhouette;
- specimen label line;
- transport emblem;
- one decorative cloud/wave/bamboo pattern;
- one tiny secondary vignette.

Supporting marks must occupy less than 15% of the ticket and must not turn the composition into a scrapbook.
