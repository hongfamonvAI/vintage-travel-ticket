# Corpus Design Index

This file distils 66 private source tickets into anonymous design observations. It contains no source image, copied destination, logo, seal, serial, or literal artwork. Use it to understand the range and internal coherence of the corpus, never to reconstruct one reference.

## How to read the index

- `H` and `V` indicate horizontal and vertical format; the number is the long-side ratio.
- `Structure` records actual information geometry, not subject matter.
- `Image` records print language and abstraction level.
- `Type` records role contrast and title behaviour, not a modern font name.
- `Age` records plausible physical or print-process evidence. Never replace it with a uniform grunge layer.

## Corpus-level findings

- The valid corpus contains **66 tickets: 37 horizontal and 29 vertical**. Horizontal ratios cluster around `2.4:1`–`3.1:1`, with one true ultra-wide example around `4.7:1`. Vertical ratios split into compact cards around `1:1.7`–`1:2.1` and long strips around `1:2.4`–`1:3.7`.
- Only a minority are built around a conventional detachable stub. Other administrative zones include blank serial thirds, value corners, bottom bands, side rails, stamp cells, title caps, seal blocks, and no explicit administrative zone at all.
- Cream stock is common but not dominant enough to be a default. The corpus also uses white, cyan, cobalt, lime, jade, pink, lavender, aubergine, charcoal, olive, ochre, and saturated green fields.
- Illustration is not one generic hand-drawn mode. The corpus spans fine engraving, naïve colour souvenir drawing, monochrome woodcut, cut-paper silhouette, schematic maps, institutional band graphics, historical figure caricature, scientific specimen plates, cartoon recreation graphics, painterly inserts, and typography-led calligraphic compositions.
- Titles range across high-contrast Song/Ming, narrow Fangsong, Kai-like lead type, clerical/seal forms, black brush calligraphy, hand-painted sign lettering, outlined recreation display, squared industrial block type, reversed white knockout, vertical Roman rails, and pure emblem lettering.
- Authentic ageing is usually selective and quiet: faded exposure, ink pinholes, registration shift, broken rules, stamp ghosts, fold dirt, soft corners, torn perforations, and rubbed solid fields. Heavy brown grunge and uniform kraft texture are not corpus norms.
