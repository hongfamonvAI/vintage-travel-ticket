# Print and Ageing System

Age the object as a printed ticket, not as a digital image under a brown texture.

## Primary ageing events

Choose two events from different physical causes.

| Event | Apply to | Visual evidence |
|---|---|---|
| dry plate | broad solids or dense lines | pinhole dropout, grainy edges, incomplete ink islands |
| low-pressure letterpress | title, numbers, thin rules | broken terminals, weak centres, variable stroke density |
| registration shift | second/third spot colour | offset contour of roughly 0.5–2 mm, never every element equally |
| sun fade | one exposed band or face | lower saturation and contrast in a directional region |
| handling rub | corners, fold ridges, protruding title strokes | selective loss where fingers or folds contact ink |
| stamp ghost | serial/value/admin zone | partial translucent seal or number offset, never decorative grunge |
| fold dirt | one actual crease | fine darker ridge plus local fibre break |
| perforation wear | detachable boundary | irregular tiny tears and softened teeth |
| edge softness | outer cut | rounded fibres, tiny nicks, uneven but restrained outline |
| plate misalignment | rule/frame or duplicate pass | faint double line or shifted border impression |

## Optional secondary events

Use zero or one:

- two to five tiny foxing spots;
- one pinhole or punched inspection dot;
- faint pencil/ink checking mark;
- mild paper translucency;
- one cropped or dirty stamp edge;
- slight long-edge bowing.

## Wear levels

| Level | Use | Limit |
|---|---|---|
| light archival | institutional banner, clean recreation graphic | ink imperfection visible only at close view; no brown cast |
| handled | default for most tickets | two clear events, readable title, lightly softened edges |
| heavily circulated | transport, coupon pair, serial tickets | fold, tear, stamp and rubbed fields allowed; keep main title intact |
| sun-faded souvenir | pale panorama, pink or cyan stock | directional colour loss; do not add heavy dirt everywhere |

## Anti-patterns

- no universal beige overlay;
- no identical scratches across paper and ink;
- no burned corners unless the source corpus family explicitly implies fire damage;
- no coffee rings, tape collage, or scrapbook dust;
- no pristine text floating over aged paper;
- no damage that makes the verified destination, user name, or date unreadable.
